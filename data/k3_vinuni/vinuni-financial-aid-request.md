# Guidelines for Student Financial Aid Support Request

This guideline explains how VinUni students can request financial support for tuition, living costs, exchange-related expenses, or emergency needs.

## Financial support programs covered

Programs listed under the Financial Aid Office include:

- VinUni Need-Based Financial Aid for regular tuition support.
- Global Exchange Student Ambassador Grant for exchange-related non-tuition expenses such as travel, visa, housing, and insurance.
- Student Support Assistance for emergency financial assistance, such as on-campus housing subsidy, travel expenses, meals, or tuition support in special cases.

The guideline states that merit-based scholarships, student research grants, start-up grants, and work-study are handled outside this document by other responsible units.

## Eligibility criteria

Students must satisfy all listed requirements, including:

- Enrollment in a full-time academic program at VinUni.
- Demonstration of financial need through supporting documentation.
- Satisfactory academic progress and not being in at-risk academic standing.
- No major disciplinary action at Level 3 or above at the time of application.
- Completion of the necessary application forms.
- Clear explanation of any special circumstances such as family emergencies or loss of income.

## Timelines

The guideline gives standard application windows:

- 20 June to 10 July, with processing deadline 02 August, for the Fall semester.
- 10 October to 10 November, with processing deadline 10 December, for the Spring semester.

Special cases may be considered. Exchange-grant timing depends on exchange nomination schedules.

## Application procedure

The process includes:

1. Gather required documents such as the application form, proof of financial need, and supporting documents.
2. Submit the request online through the student portal by the relevant deadline.
3. Initial review by the Financial Aid Office for completeness and eligibility.
4. Review by the Financial Aid Review Committee based on financial need, academic standing, available resources, special circumstances, and program eligibility.
5. Notification of decision through email or the student portal.
6. Disbursement of funds according to the type of support awarded.

## Disbursement rules

- For tuition support, approved funds are applied directly to tuition and reflected in the billing statement.
- For non-tuition support, approved funds are paid to the student's VND bank account for relevant expenses.
- If funds need to be paid to a parent or relative, the student must provide formal authorization.

## Appeals and renewal

- If a request is denied or the student wants to appeal the awarded amount, an appeal form may be submitted within five working days after the decision.
- The appeal should include a written explanation and any new supporting documentation.
- The committee provides a final appeal decision within ten working days.
- Financial support may be awarded for one semester, one academic year, or one-time aid, depending on the case.
- Renewals require a new application and updated financial documentation by the deadline.

## Contact details

The guideline gives Financial Aid Office contact information, including email, phone, location on campus, and office hours.
