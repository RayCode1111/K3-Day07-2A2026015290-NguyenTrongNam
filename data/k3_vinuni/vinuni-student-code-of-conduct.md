# Student Code of Conduct

This policy describes the standards of behavior expected from VinUniversity students, along with student rights, responsibilities, prohibited acts, and disciplinary actions.

## Purpose and scope

- The Code applies to all VinUniversity students, including undergraduate, graduate, exchange, visiting, dual-degree, and transfer students.
- It covers behavior on campus, at University-sponsored off-campus activities, in digital environments associated with the University, and off-campus conduct that affects the University community or reputation.
- The Code remains applicable throughout a student's enrollment, including breaks and periods between terms.

## Student rights

The Code lists rights including:

- Official enrollment and access to essential resources such as a student ID and student portal account.
- Fair treatment, transparency, and access to academic and student-life regulations.
- Access to learning and development support such as library resources, facilities, research opportunities, health services, exchange opportunities, counseling, and support services.
- Consideration for scholarships, financial aid, and housing according to applicable regulations.
- The right to give feedback, raise concerns, and seek support through legitimate channels.

## Student responsibilities

Responsibilities include:

- Following the laws of Vietnam and VinUni regulations.
- Studying according to the academic program and maintaining integrity.
- Respecting faculty, staff, peers, and the wider community.
- Using respectful, constructive, and good-faith communication, especially when giving feedback or raising concerns.
- Protecting University property and reputation.
- Fulfilling required payments such as tuition, health insurance, and other mandatory fees.
- Reporting misconduct or serious violations when becoming aware of them.

## Prohibited acts

The Code prohibits conduct such as:

- Harassment, threats, or infringement on the dignity or safety of others.
- Academic dishonesty including cheating, plagiarism, impersonation, or research misconduct.
- Misuse of the VinUni name or identity without approval.
- Harmful or misleading online conduct that damages the University or others.
- Alcohol or smoking violations on campus.
- Unlawful gatherings, gambling, drug-related conduct, or other illegal activities.

## Disciplinary actions

The Code identifies four main tiers of formal discipline:

- Tier 1: Expression of Disapproval
- Tier 2: Warning
- Tier 3: Temporary Suspension
- Tier 4: Dismissal or Expulsion

Warnings and higher-level actions are recorded in official student records and may be communicated to the student's family.

## Appeals

- Students who believe a disciplinary decision is unjust or incorrect may submit an appeal.
- The policy states that the written appeal with evidence must be sent within 10 working days from receipt of the decision.
