# Academic Regulations for Full-Time Undergraduate Programs

This policy sets the main academic rules for full-time undergraduate students at VinUniversity. It covers study planning, course registration, study load, add/drop/withdrawal, transfer credit, and graduation-related rules.

## Scope and general study structure

- The policy applies to full-time undergraduate students and also mentions exchange and visiting students.
- A normal annual full-time load is 30 credits.
- A full-time student must take at least 80% of a normal full-time load in a regular semester, equivalent to 12 credits.
- The academic year has two main semesters, Fall and Spring.
- Each main semester includes 15 weeks of study, 1 independent study or revision week, and 2 weeks for final exams or final assessment.

## Class times and scheduling

- Classes may begin at 8:00 a.m. and finish at 5:30 p.m. from Monday to Friday, depending on class type.
- Regular class periods normally last 50 or 75 minutes.
- Evening or weekend classes may be arranged with approval and can run until 9:25 p.m.

## Program enrollment and student responsibilities

- Students are admitted to a College or academic program and receive a student identity card after registration.
- Students with an undeclared major must declare their home major by the end of the second year.
- Students are responsible for understanding curriculum requirements and planning ahead to finish within the permitted duration.
- Unless approved otherwise, full-time students are expected to study full-time and are not allowed to enroll in another full-time or part-time award program at VinUni or another institution.

## Course registration

- The Office of Registrar announces the academic calendar, course list, course descriptions, prerequisites, exam periods, and assessment formats.
- Students must register for courses with the Office of Registrar.
- Registration must fit program requirements and prerequisite rules.
- Exceptions require approval from the relevant academic authority.

## Study load

- Normal load in a regular semester is usually at least 14 credits for first-semester students and students in good academic standing, except in the final semester.
- Students with poor academic standing in the previous semester may be limited to 10-14 credits.
- Automatic overload is 18-22 credits, subject to review by an Academic Advisor or Program Director.
- More than 22 credits requires approval from the College Dean or delegate.
- Summer study can be up to 9 credits and has no required minimum load.
- Students who want to overload in certain cases must submit a petition.

## Prerequisites

- Students must satisfy prerequisites before registering for a course.
- Exemptions from prerequisites require approval from the Program Director.

## Add, drop, and withdrawal

- Students may add a course no later than the close of the 10th business day of a regular semester.
- Students may drop a course within the published drop deadline, normally no later than the close of the 15th business day of the regular semester.
- Changes made during the drop period are not included in the academic record.
- After the add/drop period, dropping a class is considered a withdrawal.
- A withdrawn course receives a "W" on the transcript if the withdrawal happens after the drop deadline and before the course passes the allowed study-time threshold.
- Withdrawal is permitted only before a student has completed more than 30% of the study time of the course.
- Students can withdraw from a maximum of 18 credits over the entire program.

## Transfer credit and course exemption

- Transfer credit may be granted for prior study completed elsewhere at an appropriate level recognized by the University.
- Transfer credits normally do not count into GPA and appear on the transcript with grade "T".
- Total recognized transfer credit cannot exceed 50% of the minimum academic load of the program.
- Course exemption may be granted without credit transfer, but students may need to complete replacement credits.
