# Formal Escalation Management Procedure for Students' Complaint

This guideline explains how current students at VinUniversity can raise formal complaints about University actions, lack of action, or standards of service.

## Scope

- The procedure applies when a current student wants to express a complaint about the University's action or lack of action, or the standard of service provided by or on behalf of the University.
- The document covers complaints relating to the University and its colleges, departments, offices, units, and student-led activities or events under University governance.

## Principles

- Complaints should be raised respectfully, constructively, and in good faith.
- A complaint is considered genuine when the student feels the matter is not appropriate for informal resolution, or informal resolution has failed.
- Students should cooperate with the University during the complaint process.
- Anonymous complaints are not normally considered unless the University decides there is a compelling reason.
- While a complaint is under internal review, students are expected not to prematurely escalate the matter through public pressure or social media.

## Timing

- The University aims to address complaints within 40 days of receiving a complete complaint form across all three stages.
- Some matters may require quicker action, especially if there are mental-health concerns or external deadlines.
- Cases involving other internal procedures may take longer, but the complainant should be informed of delays.

## Complaint stages

The procedure states that the process has three stages:

1. Local Resolution
2. Formal Resolution
3. Review

## Student expectations during the process

- Students should have genuine grounds for complaint.
- Complaints should not be malicious or frivolous.
- Students should cooperate in seeking a fair resolution.
- Any actual or potential conflict of interest should be disclosed promptly.

## Notes useful for retrieval

- This document is ideal for queries about complaint stages, complaint timing, whether anonymous complaints are handled, and what behavior is expected while a complaint is under review.
- It pairs well with the Student Code of Conduct because both documents emphasize respectful, good-faith behavior and official internal channels.
