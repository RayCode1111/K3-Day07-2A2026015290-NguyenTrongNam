# Library Access & Services Policy

This policy explains who may access the VinUni library, how borrowing works, what users may do in library spaces, and what happens when rules are violated.

## Access and opening hours

- Only students, faculty, and staff with a valid VinUni ID may enter the library, borrow items, or use electronic resources.
- IDs must be shown when requested.
- Non-regular users such as adjunct or affiliated faculty and some eligible partner staff may apply for access according to the library website process.
- The policy states that opening hours may change during exam periods, holidays, and summer.
- The main entrance normally opens on weekdays and weekends with separate schedules, and the 24/7 learning space remains open continuously.

## Borrowing library materials

The policy distinguishes several material types:

- General books can be borrowed.
- Reference materials and journals are not available for borrowing.
- CDs and DVDs can be borrowed.
- Course reserve books can be borrowed for short-term use, normally 2 hours and 1 item per user at a time.

### Borrowing rules

- Materials must be returned or renewed before the due time.
- Overdue items cannot be renewed.
- Renewal is allowed only if no one else has requested the item.
- If the due date falls on a holiday, it is extended to the next open day.
- All loans must be returned and fines paid at least one month before leaving VinUni.

## Borrowing privileges

The policy lists user-group borrowing limits and loan periods:

- Undergraduate students: 3 items, 2 weeks, 1 renewal.
- Graduate students: 5 items, 1 month, 1 renewal.
- VinUni faculty: 5 items, 6 months, 1 renewal.
- Some guests, interns, and alumni are not eligible to borrow.

## Requests and recalls

- Borrowed items can be requested through the library process if they are checked out.
- All borrowed items may be recalled after one week.
- If an item is recalled, the borrower receives an email with a new earlier due date and should return it by that date.

## Electronic resources

- Access to library databases is limited to VinUni students, faculty, and staff unless a license states otherwise.
- Users log in with their VinUni ID.
- Materials are for personal and non-commercial use only.
- Misuse may lead to loss of access and possibly disciplinary or legal action.

## Equipment and functional rooms

- Library equipment loan period is 1 working day.
- Equipment must be returned 15 minutes before library closing time.
- Items overdue for more than 5 days are considered lost.
- Functional rooms such as group rooms or studios can be booked in advance.
- Room use is academic-only, with a maximum of 2 hours per session and 2 sessions per day across all rooms combined.

## Violations and fines

- Students who break library rules may face penalties under the Student Code of Conduct.
- Fines for late return, damage, or loss follow the Financial Regulations and Tariff.
- Fines may be waived only in serious cases such as illness or hospitalization with proof.
- Appeals of fines are handled through the library contact listed in the policy.
