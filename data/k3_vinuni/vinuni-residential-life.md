# Residential Life Guideline

This guideline helps students understand expectations for living in VinUni-provided accommodation, including community norms, residence routines, and support contacts.

## Scope and purpose

- The guideline applies to students residing in VinUni-provided accommodation, including on-campus dormitories and off-campus residences arranged by the University and its partners.
- It aims to support respectful community life, responsible choices, personal development, and active participation in the residential community.

## Community principles

The guideline promotes a shared residential culture built around:

- Openness to racial, cultural, gender, and religious diversity, and sexual orientation.
- Respect for people, property, and the environment.
- Healthy daily living and positive community behavior.
- Co-living, co-working, and co-learning as part of university life.

## Residence expectation for first-year students

- The policy states that all first-year students are required to reside in the VinUni dormitory as part of community-building objectives.

## Support network

- Students can seek support from the residential-life network for housing guidance and related issues.
- The University presents both on-campus and off-campus residential support contacts through appendices and official support channels.

## Noise policy and quiet hours

Residents are expected to avoid disturbing neighbors, especially at night. Examples include avoiding loud music, shouting, banging on doors, or creating noise when entering and leaving apartments.

The guideline gives an overview of quiet hours:

- Sunday to Thursday: 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.
- Friday to Saturday: 12:00 AM to 7:00 AM.

## Curfew and on/off-campus routine

- On-campus curfew begins after 11:00 PM, and students are expected to remain on campus after that time.
- For off-campus routine, there are no fixed hours, but students are expected to maintain a safe and healthy schedule.
- Students are expected to stay on campus as much as possible, especially during the week for classes and student activities.
- If students want to stay off campus, usually on weekends, they should email the Residential Office in advance and specify departure and return dates.
