# Guidelines for Maintaining Entry Scholarship and Financial Aid Support

This guideline explains the renewal conditions for entry merit-based scholarships and financial aid support at VinUniversity.

## Scope

- The document applies to full-time undergraduate students at VinUniversity who receive merit-based scholarships or financial aid.
- It covers renewal expectations for entry scholarships and need-based financial aid support.
- Entry merit-based scholarships are awarded for the full period of study and are not eligible for upgrade to a higher scholarship level.

## Merit-based scholarship renewal

### Full and 100% scholarships

Renewal depends on:

- Maintaining a cumulative GPA of 3.2 or higher in the academic year under evaluation, calculated from Fall and Spring.
- Maintaining good disciplinary standing, which means no major misconduct at Tier 3 or extremely serious misconduct at Tier 4 under the Student Code of Conduct and Academic Integrity rules.
- Completing the E.X.C.E.L self-evaluation and meeting with an Advisor to discuss it.

If academic performance falls below the minimum requirement:

- Automatic downgrade by one level applies if academic-year GPA is between 0.0 and 2.49.
- Conditional maintenance may apply if GPA is between 2.50 and 3.19 for full and 100% scholarships, with an extra regular semester for improvement.

### 50% to 90% scholarships

Renewal depends on:

- Maintaining a cumulative GPA of 2.5 or higher in the academic year under evaluation.
- Maintaining good disciplinary standing.
- Completing the E.X.C.E.L self-evaluation and meeting with an Advisor.

If academic-year GPA is between 0.0 and 2.49, an automatic downgrade by one level applies according to the current scholarship scheme.

## Other scholarships or aid types

- Vinschool-VinUni Scholarship: automatic renewal with no additional requirements stated here.
- Vingroup Family Scholarship: follows the current policy in effect at the time of application.
- WIT Scholarship: automatic renewal if the main entry scholarship is maintained.
- Special Academic Scholarship: automatic renewal.
- Education Development Aid: automatic renewal.

## Need-based financial aid renewal

Need-based financial aid renewal requires:

- Minimum CGPA 2.0 in the academic year under evaluation, calculated from the two main semesters.
- Completion of the E.X.C.E.L self-evaluation and Advisor discussion.
