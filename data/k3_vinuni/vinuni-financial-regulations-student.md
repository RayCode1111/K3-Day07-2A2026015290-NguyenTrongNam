# Financial Regulations and Tariff (for student)

This policy explains tuition, student fees, library fines, administrative fees, re-examination and retake fees, dormitory fees, and several student financial rules at VinUniversity.

## Tuition overview

- Tuition fees are issued according to the standard curriculum framework announced at admission.
- Tuition includes training or practical fees, facilities fees, IT support, student activities, counseling, clubs, sports and cultural activities, and related support services.
- Tuition applies equally to Vietnamese and international students.
- Students normally pay tuition twice each year at the beginning of the main semesters, according to the University announcement schedule.

## Tuition by program

- Bachelor of Nursing: 349,650,000 VND per academic year, 174,825,000 VND per semester, 9,780,000 VND per credit.
- Doctor of Medicine: 815,850,000 VND per academic year, 407,925,000 VND per semester, 27,195,000 VND per credit.
- Other bachelor programs: 815,850,000 VND per academic year, 407,925,000 VND per semester, 27,195,000 VND per credit.

## Important additional fees

### Application and confirmation-related fees

- Application fee: 2,000,000 VND per application or applicant in cases specified by the policy.
- Retention or confirmation fee for scholarship, tuition discount, or financial-aid eligibility: 20,000,000 VND per student each time.

### Library fines and damage fees

- Normal material overdue fine: 10,000 VND per day per document.
- Course reserve material overdue fine: 10,000 VND per hour per document.
- Equipment overdue fine: 10,000 VND per day per equipment item.
- Students with unpaid overdue fines cannot continue borrowing library materials or equipment.
- If books are returned more than 30 days late, additional replacement-related charges may apply.
- Slight damage repair fee: 200,000 VND per document or equipment item.
- Lost or badly damaged library documents or equipment must be paid according to replacement rules plus administrative fees in some cases.

## Academic administrative fees

Examples listed in the policy include:

- Student ID replacement: 200,000 VND per card.
- Certified academic documents: 50,000 VND per copy.
- Processing tuition deferment or refund request: 2,000,000 VND per request.
- Processing transfer between programs: 2,000,000 VND per request.
- Processing concurrent enrollment in two programs: 2,000,000 VND per request.
- Credit transfer evaluation: 2,000,000 VND per requested subject.
- Exam score review: 1,000,000 VND per request per subject.

## Re-examination and retake fees

- Retaken credits are charged at 50% of the standard per-credit tuition fee.
- Re-examination fees:
  - Theoretical exam: 1,500,000 VND per attempt.
  - Practical exam: 2,500,000 VND per attempt.
  - NBME-standard exam: 2,000,000 VND per attempt.
- For students admitted before academic year 2025, the first retake or re-examination of courses initially registered before Fall 2025 remains free under the conditions described by the policy.
- For students admitted from Fall 2025 onward, the new fee policy applies fully to all retake or re-examination attempts.

## Dormitory fees

- Dormitory fees include accommodation, facilities, internet, common restroom area, technical fee, parking for one vehicle per student, and electricity and water within stated norms.
- The fee is based on days of residence and official move-in and move-out dates.
- Standard rates listed in the policy range from 3,500,000 VND to 5,000,000 VND per person per month depending on building and room type.
- A preferential returning-resident rate exists if the student meets the stated conditions.
- Each dormitory student must pay a deposit equal to one month of rent.

## Financial obligations and service restrictions

- Students with outstanding non-tuition fees may face restrictions on access to certain academic and student services.
- Students need to fulfill all financial obligations before being considered for graduation.
